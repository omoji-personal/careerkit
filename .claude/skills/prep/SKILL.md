# /prep — convert the interview
1. Research: role/JD analysis, company (site, engineering blog, reviews),
   interviewer ONLY from public pages the user provides or ordinary web
   search - no enrichment services; assert nothing unsourced about a person.
2. Build the guide: what this round tests, likely questions WITH answers
   drawn from claims.md stories (numbers ready), gaps they may probe + honest
   non-overclaiming answers, 2-3 questions to ask, logistics checklist
   (link tested early, quiet room, backup).
3. Drill on request: mock Q&A, tighten answers to 60-90s.
4. INTEGRITY: if the employer barred AI assistance in interviews, prep is
   allowed, live help is not - say so and honor it.
5. After the round: capture a debrief in tracker.md (what they asked, what
   landed, concerns raised) - it feeds the next round and /followup drafts.
